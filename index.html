What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research worksheet for keeping links, categories, sizing notes, inspection references and estimated costs together. Use it to compare options consistently; inclusion in a list does not verify a seller, product or brand affiliation.

Copy these fields into your own worksheet to create a clear, repeatable comparison process.

Suggested fields for a fashion product research spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it matters
Product reference Listing URL, seller, category and selected color or version Helps you return to the same item and variant.
Fit and sizing Size chart, measurement units and garment or footwear dimensions Supports comparison with something you already own.
Materials Stated composition, lining, trim and care instructions Makes product descriptions easier to compare.
Inspection notes Available photographs, visible details and unresolved questions Separates what you can observe from what remains unverified.
Total cost Item price, delivery, applicable fees, shipping and taxes Creates a more useful budget than item price alone.
Last checked Date reviewed, availability and return conditions Shows which details may need checking again.

05 / Clear answers

build your list.

Straightforward answers about this guide, product research and the limits of a spreadsheet.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, it means a worksheet used to organize product links, categories, size notes, inspection references and estimated costs while researching a purchase. A listing in a spreadsheet does not verify a product or seller.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim an official relationship with Litbuy or any featured brand.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

This page provides a browsing guide and a suggested worksheet structure. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment you already own. Record the units, chest or waist width, length and other relevant measurements instead of relying only on a letter size.

Do product photographs prove authenticity?

No. Photographs can help you inspect visible details but do not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s evidence, purchase terms and available authentication options.

What should a total cost estimate include?

Include the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service and payment fees, international shipping, and any applicable duties or taxes. Confirm current charges with the relevant service before paying.
